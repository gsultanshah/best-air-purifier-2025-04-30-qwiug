Frequently Asked Questions

Filter replacement varies based on usage and air quality. Generally, HEPA filters should be replaced every 6-12 months, while pre-filters need cleaning every 2-3 months. Our smart indicator will notify you when it's time for replacement.

Our purifiers are designed for rooms ranging from 200 to 800 square feet. The Dyson model covers up to 800 sq ft, while the Levoit is ideal for spaces up to 500 sq ft. For optimal performance, we recommend choosing a model rated for slightly larger than your room size.

Our purifiers are specifically designed to handle UAE's sandstorms. They feature enhanced pre-filtration systems and automatically increase power during high dust conditions. The smart sensors detect minute particles and adjust performance accordingly.

All our purifiers come with a comprehensive 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and mechanical failures. This includes the motor, electronic components, and sensors. Filters are consumable items and not covered under warranty.
